Don't take our word for it.

Poof is a few steps inside Apple's Shortcuts app. You can read every one of them.

Read the recipe

  • Open Poof in the Shortcuts app. You will see one Convert Image action with Preserve Metadata switched off, then Share.
  • There is no network action, no “Get Contents of URL”, no analytics.
  • Apple's own description of that switch: “all metadata, such as the GPS coordinates where the photo was taken, will be stripped.”

How we test it

  • A checker flags any GPS, date, camera or text-chunk data. We first prove it catches leaks in a deliberately leaky test photo.
  • Automated tests run on every change. Real-phone tests are listed in the phone test plan.
  • Any leak counts as a release-blocking bug.

Which photos can it see?

  • Poof only receives the photos you tap Share on. It does not ask for access to your library.
  • Poof-Pick uses Apple's photo picker: only photos you select.
  • Poof-Latest reads your newest photo, so iOS may ask you to allow Photos access the first time.

Privacy, plainly

  • Everything runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
  • This site has no analytics, cookies or third-party requests.
  • Your original photos are never changed.

Questions.

Why does it ask “Allow … to share with other apps?”

Apple shows this the first time a shortcut passes a photo to the Share sheet. Tap Always Allow and it stops asking. Poof has no upload step. You can open it in Shortcuts and read it: Convert Image, then Share.

The cleaned photo shows today's date. Did it work?

Yes. With the original date removed, Photos shows the time the copy was saved instead. Check that camera and location are empty too.

Do I need all four shortcuts?

No. Install Poof. The other three are optional extras.

Does it change my original photo?

No. Poof works on a copy it makes while sharing. Your library keeps its location, date and everything else.

Will the photo look worse?

It is re-saved without the hidden data, so the file size can differ slightly. We are checking image quality and orientation on real devices and will report results here.

Why a Shortcut and not an app?

Shortcuts is already on your iPhone, so there is nothing to install from the App Store and no account. It also keeps everything on your device.

Does it need internet?

It shouldn't. Every step runs on the phone. We will confirm this in airplane mode during device testing.

What is “metadata” (EXIF)?

Hidden facts saved inside a photo file: where it was taken, when, and with which phone. You can't see them in the picture, but apps and people who receive the file can.

Do I need Poof-Peek?

Not to use Poof. It's for checking: run it on a photo before and after to see what was hiding there.

Found a leak, a bug or something confusing? Report a problem on GitHub. A metadata leak is treated as a release-blocking bug.

Poof removes metadata (location, date, device), not what is visible in the picture. Your originals in Photos are never touched.
