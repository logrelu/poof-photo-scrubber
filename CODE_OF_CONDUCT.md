# Code of Conduct

Be kind. Poof exists to protect people's privacy, so we treat everyone here with the same care.

## What we expect
- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Never share someone else's personal information, including location or photos, without their permission. Don't post real photos with real location data in issues. Use the sample photo instead.

## What we don't accept
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, hide or delete comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break these rules.

To report a problem privately, use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and mark it as a conduct issue, or open a minimal issue asking a maintainer to contact you without details.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
